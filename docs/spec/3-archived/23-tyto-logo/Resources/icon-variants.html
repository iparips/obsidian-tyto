Symmetry

Every path here is generated by authoring the left half and mirroring it about x=50, then verified by rendering and comparing each row of pixels against its mirror. All ten variants measure under 0.5 mean difference on a 0-255 scale, which is antialiasing noise rather than drift. The earlier hand-typed paths measured 6-17 and were visibly lopsided.
